Privacy policy

This explains what Fondly collects, why, who sees it, and how to have it removed. We keep it to what the service needs.

What we collect

  • What you put in a gift: names, your message, and any photos, video or song you add. Photos and videos can show people, so please only use ones you have permission to share.
  • Your contact details: your email address (we send your link and any replies there), and your mobile number if you choose to give it (only so we can help with an order).
  • Payment details: we do not see or store your bank or card details. We only see that a payment of a certain amount reached our account, with its UPI reference number. We keep that with your order.
  • Internet (IP) address: when you place an order, and when someone opens a gift, but only if you chose "Tell me when it is opened". We compare the two to tell whether the gift was opened from a different place. We keep the IP addresses with the order, and use them for nothing else.
  • Replies: a message the person receiving a gift chooses to send back (and the name they choose to give).

Why we use it

To make your gift, show it to the person you send the link to, take and check your payment, email you (your link, a reminder if your payment cannot be found, a reply, or an "opened" notice), and help you if something goes wrong. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Google runs the systems we use to store orders (Google Sheets), your photos, videos and songs (Google Drive), and to send and receive our email (Gmail and Google Apps Script). Google's own privacy terms apply to that storage.
  • ipify (api.ipify.org) tells your browser its own IP address, when you order and when a gift with notifications on is opened. ipify sees that request.
  • GitHub hosts the website pages.
  • Your photos, videos and songs are shared by an unguessable link so the person you send the gift to can see them. Anyone who has the gift link can see the gift.

How long we keep it

A gift link works for one year from the day your payment is confirmed. We keep order records (without the content of your gift) for as long as we need them for accounts and tax. You can ask us to delete your gift and its files at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to switch your gift off, by emailing fondlyshop.in@gmail.com from the address you ordered with. We reply within 1 to 2 working days. You can turn "Tell me when it is opened" off when you make your gift.

Children

Fondly is meant for adults to buy. Please do not enter a child's personal details unless you are their parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes. Questions: contact us.
