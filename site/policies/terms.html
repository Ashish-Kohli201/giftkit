Terms of use

These terms apply when you use Fondly (the website and the gifts you make on it). By paying for a gift you agree to them. They are written in plain words on purpose.

What Fondly is

Fondly lets you make a small digital gift (a sealed envelope, a cake to blow out, a memory film, rolling credits) with your own words, photos, video and song. You get a private link, and you send it to the person it is for. A gift is a web page. Nothing is posted to anyone.

Your order

  • The price you see, including any add-ons you choose, is the price you pay, in rupees. There are no hidden charges.
  • You pay by UPI to our UPI ID, for the exact amount shown (the few extra paise, if any, are how we find your payment).
  • Your gift link opens as soon as you tell us you have paid. We then check for your payment. If we cannot find it, we email you a reminder, and if it still has not arrived the link is switched off. If your payment reaches us later, the link switches back on by itself.
  • Please only tell us you have paid after you have paid.

What you may put in a gift

You must own, or have permission to use, every word, photo, video and song you add. You must not add anything that is illegal, hateful, threatening, harassing, sexually explicit, or that shows a person in a way they have not agreed to. You must not use a gift to cheat, scare or bother anyone. We may switch off a gift that breaks these rules, and we may refuse or refund an order, without notice.

The link and how long it lasts

Your gift link is private: only people who have the link can open it. It is not searchable. It is not protected by a password, so anyone you send it to can pass it on. A gift link works for one year from the day your payment is confirmed.

Replies and notifications

The person who receives your gift can send you a short message back at the end of it, and we email it to you. If you choose "Tell me when it is opened", we email you the first two times someone on a different network opens your gift. To do this we compare the internet (IP) address of the person opening the gift with the one you ordered from. See the privacy policy.
