Refund and cancellation policy

A Fondly gift is a digital product that is made for you and delivered straight away as a link. Because of that, the rules below are short and fair.

How your payment is checked

Your gift link opens as soon as you tell us you have paid, and we confirm your payment against our bank records, usually within a few minutes. If we cannot see your payment, we email you a reminder after 5 minutes, and the link is switched off after 10 minutes. If you did pay, reply to that email with a screenshot of the payment (the UPI reference number is the most useful) and we switch the link straight back on. A payment that reaches us late switches the link back on by itself.

When you get a refund

  • We could not deliver your gift. Your payment reached us, but a fault on our side meant your gift could not be made or opened, and we could not fix it.
  • You paid twice for the same gift, or you paid an amount different from the one shown.
  • Your gift does not match what you made because of an error on our side, and we could not correct it.

In each case, write to us within 7 days of paying. Once we have checked and approved your request, we send the money back to the UPI ID or bank account it came from within 24 to 48 hours. After that, your bank may take a few more working days to show it.

When we cannot refund

  • You changed your mind after your gift was delivered. The gift is made for you and cannot be returned.
  • The link was shared and opened by the person you sent it to.
  • The gift was switched off because it broke our terms.

Cancelling

You can close the page at any time before you pay, and nothing is charged. After you have paid, a gift cannot be cancelled, but you can ask us to switch your gift off, and to delete it and its files, by writing to us.

How to ask

Email fondyshop@gmail.com from the email address you used when you ordered, and include your order number (it is on your confirmation and on the page you saw after paying). We reply within 1 to 2 working days.
